On the Persistence of Objects
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics · Draft

What does it take for an object to persist through change? This essay sketches a four-dimensionalist reply and asks whether our ordinary talk about tables and persons can be reconciled with a spacetime worm picture without giving up too much of common sense.

Knowledge and Luck
Angelina Quan
Epistemology · Notes

A short treatment of Gettier cases and the role of safety and sensitivity in a theory of knowledge. I argue that a modest virtue-theoretic constraint helps block some familiar counterexamples while staying close to everyday ascriptions of knowing.

Moral Reasons and Partiality
Angelina Quan
Ethics · Work in progress

Are we permitted—or required—to favor friends and family in how we act? I compare impartialist and relational accounts of moral reason and suggest that partiality is best understood as a constraint on how reasons aggregate, not as an exception to morality itself.
