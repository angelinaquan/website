Philosophy Essays

As a philosophy minor, I've taken a bunch of classes where the best part was arguing about stuff with no right answer. This page has some of that - either coursework or essays I write for fun.

Predicating Properties of Non-Existents
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics

If something has a property, does it exist? I argue not always - fictional and modal discourse let us ascribe properties without the subject existing in the actual world.

Instantaneous Velocity Is Not at an Instant
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics

Velocity is usually treated as a property at an instant. I argue it is really a relation over time - and the limit definition does not change that metaphysically.

Presentism and the Relativity of Simultaneity
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics

Presentism needs an objective present. I argue special relativity's relativity of simultaneity makes that hard to defend.

Frankfurt Cases and Alternative Possibilities
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics

Can you act freely without being able to do otherwise? I argue Frankfurt cases show you can—what matters is the source of the action.

Newton's Bucket and Relationalism
Angelina Quan
Metaphysics

Newton's spinning bucket is supposed to show that absolute space is real. I argue, with Dasgupta (2015), that relationalism can explain the water's curvature without it.

Everett and the Measurement Problem
Angelina Quan
Philosophy of Quantum Mechanics

Does the Everett interpretation solve the measurement problem? I argue it keeps the formalism clean but leaves definiteness of experience, probability, and evidence unclear.

Is Bohm Just Everett in Disguise?
Angelina Quan
Philosophy of Quantum Mechanics

Brown and Wallace claim Bohmian mechanics already contains Everettian worlds. I argue the particle configuration still does essential explanatory work.

Configuration Space and the Quantum State
Angelina Quan
Philosophy of Quantum Mechanics

Should the quantum state be read as a field in 3N-dimensional configuration space? I argue the view is coherent but fails to non-circularly recover three-dimensional space.

Sleeping Beauty and Self-Locating Probability
Angelina Quan
Paradox and Infinity

When Beauty wakes up, should her credence in Heads be 1/2 or 1/3? I argue the Thirder view better fits her self-locating evidence.

Cantor's Theorem and the Failure of Listing Infinity
Angelina Quan
Paradox and Infinity

Is Cantor's theorem just about larger infinities? I argue it shows why no list can exhaust all subsets of a set.
