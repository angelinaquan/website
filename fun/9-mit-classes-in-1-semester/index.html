9 MIT Classes in 1 Semester

← Back to Fun

6.S058, 6.3100, 18.650, 18.619, 5.111, 24.111, 24.118, 24.137, 21T.221.

111 units. 111 hours a week in classes and labs, doing problem sets, or taking exams. Thankfully there’s 168 hours in a week!

On top of all of these, I was working as an undergraduate researcher for a PhD, and our paper ended up getting published in NeurIPS.

Why?

I wanted to push beyond the limits of what I think I can do.

If I just took 5 classes and call it a day, I’ll never know how much I can truly do. Turns out, my limit was 9 (with research on the side) for a healthy mind.

Moreover, one of the reasons why I love MIT is that I love learning. During this semester, I genuinely learned so much interesting concepts:

  • In philosophy of quantum mechanics, I learned about Everett’s interpretation for the measurement problem.
  • In control design, I learned how to build and tune controllers for a physical robotics system.
  • In lighting design, I learned how to control stage lights and controlled the lights for a section of a concert.
  • In principles of chemical science, I learned all about thermodynamics and acid-base equilibriums.
  • More and more.

How?

Sadly, I was doing the bare minimum to learn all the content and just get an A in each class. If I had to do it again, I wish I could have spent more time thinking on my own about the content in each class.

I was skipping as many classes as I could in order to learn all classes’ content on my own time, taking makeup-exams because classes conflict, spending weekends in lab, etc.

I also made friends along the way and learned how to keep a healthy balance (research was very fun to me and did not feel like work).

The one thing that I felt missing from this period of time though, was introspection. I was always going to classes, hanging out with friends, doing more work, and that let me having little time to hang by myself and really reflect.

Overall, it was a good experience and if I had to take 9 classes again, I’d probably take a lot more CS/Math classes that I’m especially interested in now.
